# LogicalArchitecture

Logical architecture of the electric car. Car is the assembly. Parts are logical, not yet software, electronics or metal. Three controllers decide: vehicleController owns power modes and authentication, driveController owns torque, braking, gear and steering, bodyController owns locks, doors, windows, lamps and wipers. Driver controls talk to a controller, controllers talk to actuators, and the instrument cluster tells the driver what happened. Dependencies show what commands what, what supplies energy and what is mounted on what. Interfaces name the contracts: a provider port (Out) and a consumer port (In), joined by a connection. The physical architecture realizes these parts with «implements».
