# UseCases

Use cases for the electric car, split into Driving (what the driver does on the road) and Managing (what driver and passengers do with the car when it stands still). Actors are the driver, the passenger and the external charger. Drive includes starting the car and the manoeuvres it is made of. Park includes stopping and locking. EmergencyStop extends Brake. Each use case names the requirement it pursues and the user story it refines.
