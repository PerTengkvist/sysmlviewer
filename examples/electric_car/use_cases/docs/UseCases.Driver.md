# Driver

The person who operates the car: unlocks and locks it, selects gear, steers, accelerates, brakes, charges and parks.

Takes part in: `Unlock`, `Lock`, `ChangeGear`, `TurnLeft`, `TurnRight`, `Brake`, `Accelerate`, `EmergencyStop`, `Charge`, `AdjustClimate`, `PlayMedia`, `CleanWindshield`, `Drive`, `Park`.
