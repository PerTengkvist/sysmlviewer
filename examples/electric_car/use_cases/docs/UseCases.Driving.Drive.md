# Drive

The driver takes the car from origin to destination. Includes starting, gear selection, speed control and steering. The passenger rides along. Speed control and steering repeat until the car has arrived.

## Relations

- Pursues requirement `Range`.
- Includes `StartVehicle`.
- Includes `ChangeGear`.
- Includes `Accelerate`.
- Includes `Brake`.
- Includes `TurnLeft`.
- Includes `TurnRight`.
- Refines `DriveToDestination`.
