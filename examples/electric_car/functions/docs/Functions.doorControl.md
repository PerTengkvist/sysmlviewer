# doorControl

Own the doors: read the inside and outside handles, open a door once its latch is free, report each door open or closed, and tell the lock whether every door is shut. Allocated to the body controller.

Part of `Security`.

## Relations

- Allocated to `bodyController`.
- Refines `OpenDoor`.
- Refines `CloseDoor`.
- Refines `Lock`.
