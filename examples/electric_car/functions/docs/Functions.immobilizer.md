# immobilizer

Keep propulsion inhibited from the moment the car is switched off until an approved key starts it again; unlocking the doors does not release it. Allocated to the vehicle controller.

Part of `Security`.

- Allocated to `vehicleController`.
- Refines `StartVehicle`.
- Refines `StopVehicle`.
